# Quick Reference: GitHub Skills Pitch Deck

## One-Liner Slogans (Use for Title Slide)

Choose the one that best fits your audience:

1. **"Master the Fundamentals, Elevate Your Expertise"**
2. **"Where Learning Meets Real-World Impact"**
3. **"Transform Learning Into Action with GitHub Skills"**
4. **"The Bridge from Learning to Mastery"**
5. **"Building Excellence Through Hands-On Practice"**

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

*"GitHub Skills provides open-source, hands-on exercises that serve as prerequisites for our expert workshops. Your team completes self-paced exercises in topics aligned with your workshop goals, ensuring everyone arrives with baseline knowledge. Then our expert workshops dive deeper, customized to your specific stack, environment, and challenges. It's the bridge from learning to mastery."*

## Key Messages

### For Customers
- ✅ Free, open-source learning for your entire team
- ✅ Self-paced exercises fit your schedule
- ✅ Proven foundation before expert workshops
- ✅ Maximize value from workshop time

### For Service Delivery Engineers
- ✅ Ensures consistent baseline knowledge
- ✅ Allows focus on advanced, customized content
- ✅ Reduces time explaining basics
- ✅ Improves workshop outcomes

## The Flow (Condensed)

```
Scoping → Learn Scope → Learning Journey → Learnings
    ↓
Hands-On Practice (GitHub Skills) → Expert Workshops
```

## Common Objections & Responses

**Q: "Our team doesn't have time for prerequisites"**
A: GitHub Skills exercises are self-paced and can be completed in small chunks. Most exercises take 15-60 minutes. The time invested ensures your team maximizes the value from our expert workshops.

**Q: "Can't we just cover basics in the workshop?"**
A: We can, but that means less time for the deep, customized content that addresses your specific challenges. Prerequisites allow us to dive straight into advanced topics tailored to your environment.

**Q: "Are these exercises relevant to our stack?"**
A: We select specific GitHub Skills exercises that align with your workshop topics and goals. They provide foundational knowledge that applies regardless of your specific stack.

3. **Emphasize Benefits**: Both time savings and better outcomes
4. **Be Specific**: Mention which Skills exercises align with their workshop
5. **Make it Collaborative**: Involve customers in selecting exercises
